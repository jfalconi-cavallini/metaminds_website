# Mathematics

---

## Overview

Mathematics at MetaMinds follows the US standard curriculum sequence, enriched with SAT/ACT alignment at the high school level. Every math course is built around skill mastery, not grade completion.

---

## Math Progression

```
Elementary Math (Grades 1–5)
        ↓
Pre-Algebra (Grade 6–7)
        ↓
Algebra 1 (Grade 7–8)
        ↓
Geometry (Grade 8–9)
        ↓
Algebra 2 (Grade 9–10)
        ↓
Trigonometry (Grade 10–11)
        ↓
Pre-Calculus (Grade 11–12)
        ↓
AP Calculus AB / BC (Grade 12)
```

SAT Math spans Algebra 1 through Algebra 2 + basic Geometry.
