Geometry develops a student's ability to reason logically and think spatially. It introduces formal proof — arguably the most important mathematical skill for advanced math and computer science. Geometry content appears directly on both the SAT and ACT.

## Common Student Struggles

1. **Formal proof** — Two-column proofs are unlike anything in Algebra; the logical format is new
2. **Circle theorems** — There are many, they look similar, and students mix them up
3. **Special right triangles** — Students reach for a calculator instead of memorizing the ratios
4. **CPCTC** — Using congruent triangle proofs to conclude about parts
5. **Volume vs. surface area** — Applying the wrong formula family
6. **Trig setup** — Identifying which side is opposite/adjacent to the given angle
