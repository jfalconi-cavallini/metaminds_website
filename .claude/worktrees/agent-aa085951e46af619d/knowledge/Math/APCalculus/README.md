AP Calculus is the capstone of the high school math sequence and one of the highest-value AP exams for STEM students. AB covers limits, derivatives, and integrals (single variable). BC extends AB with sequences/series, parametric/polar, and more advanced integration.

**Most MetaMinds students taking AP Calculus are targeting a 4 or 5.** Tutoring should reflect this standard.

## FRQ Strategy

AP Calculus Free Response problems follow predictable patterns. Key tutoring focus:

1. **Show all work** — Partial credit is given; an answer with no work gets 0
2. **Justify answers** — "The function has a minimum at x=2 because f'(2)=0 and f''(2)>0"
3. **Units matter** — Velocity, acceleration, area, volume units must be stated
4. **Notation precision** — Write `f'(x)`, not `dy/dx` when the problem uses function notation

---

## Common Student Struggles

1. **Chain rule on complex compositions** — Students miss the inner derivative
2. **Related rates setup** — Drawing the diagram and identifying all relationships
3. **u-substitution choosing u** — No algorithm for this; students need pattern recognition
4. **Disk vs. washer vs. shell** — Identifying which method is appropriate
5. **FTC Part 1 vs. Part 2** — Students confuse the two applications
6. **Series convergence tests (BC)** — Many tests, students choose the wrong one
7. **Taylor series error bounds (BC)** — Alternating series estimation theorem is subtle
