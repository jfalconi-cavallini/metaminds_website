Algebra 1 is the gateway course in mathematics. Students who master Algebra 1 have the conceptual foundation to tackle every math course that follows — Geometry, Algebra 2, Pre-Calculus, and the SAT. Students who pass without understanding accumulate debt they repay over years of tutoring.

MetaMinds Algebra 1 focuses on genuine mastery: students demonstrate they can *explain why*, not just *produce the answer*.

## SAT Alignment

Algebra 1 content appears extensively on the SAT:
- ~30% of SAT Math is direct Algebra 1 content
- Systems of equations (Module 5) is a top-frequency SAT topic
- Quadratics (Modules 8–9) is critical for the Advanced Math section
- Linear functions (Module 4) appears in nearly every SAT

Students preparing for SAT should review all Algebra 1 modules before starting SAT prep.

## Common Student Struggles

1. **PEMDAS errors** — Students rush past the order of operations on multi-step problems
2. **Negative signs in distribution** — Forgetting to distribute the negative when removing parentheses
3. **Slope formula** — Mixing up rise/run or computing it backwards
4. **Factoring trinomials** — The trial-and-error of `ax² + bx + c` when `a ≠ 1`
5. **Word problems** — Writing equations from sentences; connecting algebra to real-world context
6. **Systems of equations** — Students can solve algebraic systems but not word problem systems
