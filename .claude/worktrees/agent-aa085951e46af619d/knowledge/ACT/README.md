The ACT and SAT test similar skills but in very different formats. Some students score 200+ points higher on the ACT than the SAT. Every MetaMinds student preparing for college admissions should take a diagnostic of both to determine which is their better test.

Key differences from the SAT:
- ACT has 4 sections + optional Writing (vs. 2 SAT sections)
- ACT Math includes Trigonometry (SAT does not)
- ACT Science is about data interpretation, not science facts
- ACT Reading is faster-paced (35 min for 40 questions)
- ACT English tests grammar in longer passages

---

## ACT Structure

| Section | Questions | Time | Topics |
|---------|-----------|------|--------|
| English | 75 | 45 min | Grammar, punctuation, sentence structure, rhetoric |
| Math | 60 | 60 min | Pre-Algebra through Trig, some Stats |
| Reading | 40 | 35 min | Fiction, social science, humanities, natural science |
| Science | 40 | 35 min | Data interpretation, experiments, conflicting viewpoints |
| Writing (Optional) | 1 essay | 40 min | Argumentative writing |
| **Total** | **215 (216 w/essay)** | **175 min (215 w/essay)** | |

Score range: 1–36 composite (average of 4 sections)

## Module Structure

### English Modules

#### Module 1: Grammar & Usage

Lessons:
1. Subject-Verb Agreement (difficulty: 2)
2. Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement (difficulty: 2)
3. Verb Tense & Mood (difficulty: 3)
4. Comparisons & Modifiers (difficulty: 3)

**Duration:** 1–2 sessions

1. Commas (difficulty: 2)
2. Semicolons & Colons (difficulty: 3)
3. Dashes & Parentheses (difficulty: 2)

## ACT vs. SAT: Which Is Right?

Use this table when advising a student:

| Factor | Choose ACT | Choose SAT |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Math comfort | Likes trig | Prefers no trig |
| Reading pace | Fast reader | Deliberate reader |
| Science interest | Enjoys science | N/A |
| Grammar focus | Wants lots of grammar | Prefers less |
| Essay required | Optional | Not required |
| School preference | Midwest/South colleges | East/West coast schools |

**Our advice:** Take a full official diagnostic of each. Compare percentile rankings, not raw scores.

## Common Student Struggles

1. **ACT Science intimidation** — Students think they need to know biology, chemistry, physics. They don't — it's data interpretation
2. **ACT Reading time pressure** — 52 seconds per question with 4 long passages is brutal for slow readers
3. **ACT Math covers Trig** — Students who skipped trig are unprepared
4. **Grammar volume** — 75 English questions is a lot; students fatigue by the end
5. **No calculator for SAT, calculator allowed for ACT Math** — Different strategy for each
