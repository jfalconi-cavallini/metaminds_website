Algebra 2 is where high school math gets real. Students encounter complex numbers, polynomial functions, logarithms, and an introduction to limits. Mastery here is essential for Pre-Calculus and, more immediately, for the SAT Advanced Math section. Students who struggle on the SAT at the 1200+ level are almost always weak in Algebra 2 content.

## SAT Alignment

Algebra 2 content dominates the SAT Advanced Math section:
- Quadratics, complex solutions (Module 3)
- Polynomial functions (Module 4)
- Rational equations (Module 5)
- Exponential functions (Module 6)
- Function transformations (Module 1)

Students targeting 1300+ on the SAT should have solid Algebra 2 fundamentals.

---

## Common Student Struggles

1. **Logarithms** — The switch from exponential to logarithmic form confuses most students
2. **Complex numbers** — The concept of `i` feels abstract without visual grounding
3. **Asymptotes in rational functions** — Students mix up vertical, horizontal, and oblique
4. **Completing the square** — The procedural steps are correct but the conceptual "why" is unclear
5. **Finding zeros of higher-degree polynomials** — Combining Rational Root Theorem + synthetic division
6. **Combinations vs. permutations** — Students misidentify which to use in word problems
