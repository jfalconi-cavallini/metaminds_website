Pre-Calculus synthesizes everything from Algebra 2 and Trigonometry and introduces the foundational concepts Calculus depends on: limits (conceptually), analytic geometry, and advanced function analysis. A student who earns an A in Pre-Calculus but truly understands the material will have a much easier Calculus experience.

## Common Student Struggles

1. **Trig identities** — There are many and they look interchangeable; students need a conceptual anchor
2. **Unit circle memorization** — Can be replaced with derivation, which is more durable
3. **Limits** — The formal definition is counterintuitive; students need numerical/graphical exploration first
4. **Inverse trig functions** — The restricted domain is counterintuitive
5. **Partial fraction decomposition** — Multi-step with multiple sub-cases
6. **Graphing sinusoidal functions** — All four parameters must be correct simultaneously
