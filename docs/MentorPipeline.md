# MetaMinds Mentor Pipeline

**The defining differentiator of MetaMinds.**

Most education companies hire tutors. MetaMinds grows them.

---

## Overview

The MetaMinds Mentor Pipeline is a structured leadership pathway that transforms students into educators. A student who joins at age 10 can, by age 24, be a Graduate Mentor shaping the next generation of learners — and writing the curriculum they learned from.

```
Elementary Student (ages 6–10)
        ↓
Middle School Student (ages 11–14)
        ↓
High School Student (ages 15–18)
        ↓
Junior Mentor (ages 16–18, high school)
        ↓
College Tutor (ages 18–22, undergraduate)
        ↓
Graduate Mentor (ages 22+, professional)
```

---

## Level 0: Student

**Who:** Any student enrolled in MetaMinds tutoring  
**Ages:** 6–22  
**Relationship:** Learner  

Students are the core of the platform. Every other level exists to serve them. A student at any age should feel:

- Seen as an individual, not a number
- Supported with a personalized learning path
- Celebrated for progress, not just grades
- Connected to a community of peers who share their interests
- Aware that MetaMinds will grow with them as they grow

**Student rights:**
- Personalized learning path
- Dedicated tutor
- Full access to knowledge base for their subjects
- AI practice tools
- Community access (MetaMinds Lab)
- Progress analytics visible to student and parent

## Level 1: Junior Mentor

**Who:** High school students (typically 10th–12th grade) who demonstrate mastery  
**Subjects they teach:** Elementary and middle school subjects (subjects at least 2 grade levels below their own)  
**Alternative titles:** STEM Coach, Student Instructor, Mentor-in-Training  

### The Story

A high school junior who has taken AP Computer Science, loves Scratch, and scored 1500 on their SAT. They apply to become a Junior Mentor. They go through training. They teach Scratch to 8-year-olds.

For the 8-year-old: "She's in high school and built this game!" That's inspiration.  
For the Junior Mentor: Leadership experience, teaching skills, and MetaMinds Credits that reduce the cost of their own SAT preparation.

### Requirements to Become a Junior Mentor

1. **Academic standing:** Current high school student (10th grade or above) in good standing
2. **Subject mastery:** Complete the MetaMinds curriculum for the subject(s) they wish to teach + pass a competency assessment (minimum 85%)
3. **Shadow sessions:** Observe 3 sessions with an experienced tutor in the subject
4. **Practice lessons:** Teach 2 supervised practice lessons reviewed by a Senior Tutor
5. **Approval:** Receive written approval from MetaMinds admin
6. **Training:** Complete MetaMinds Leadership Training (online, ~4 hours)
7. **Background clearance:** Required. Junior Mentors are minors themselves; sessions are always supervised or recorded.

### What Junior Mentors Teach

| Subject | Minimum JM Grade Level |
|---------|----------------------|
| Scratch Programming | 8th grade + Scratch certification |
| Elementary Math | 9th grade + competency assessment |
| LEGO Robotics | 9th grade + robotics experience |
| Reading & Writing Foundations | 10th grade + ELA assessment |
| STEM Exploration | 9th grade |

Junior Mentors do **not** teach SAT prep, AP subjects, or high school math. Those require a College Tutor or higher.

### MetaMinds Credit System

Instead of a direct paycheck (Junior Mentors are often minors and the legal/tax complexity is high), Junior Mentors earn **MetaMinds Credits**.

| Activity | Credits Earned |
|----------|---------------|
| 1 teaching session (1 hour) | 1 Credit |
| Completing a curriculum contribution | 2 Credits |
| Receiving a 5-star parent review | 1 Bonus Credit |
| Completing Leadership Training module | 1 Credit |
| Mentoring another Junior Mentor | 2 Credits |

**Credit Redemption:**

| Credits | Redemption Value |
|---------|-----------------|
| 4 Credits | 1 Free Group Session (SAT/ACT) |
| 8 Credits | 1 Free Private Tutoring Session |
| 20 Credits | SAT Bootcamp enrollment |
| 30 Credits | Programming Course enrollment |
| 50 Credits | Summer Camp enrollment |
| 100 Credits | Convert to $50 cash (age 18+ only, pending full tutor status) |

Credits never expire. They transfer when a Junior Mentor becomes a College Tutor (100 credits = $50 toward tutor onboarding fee waiver).

### Junior Mentor Protections

- All sessions recorded or conducted with a parent present
- Junior Mentors cannot manage their own schedule — admin assigns all sessions
- Junior Mentors cannot communicate with parents directly — all parent communication goes through MetaMinds
- Sessions limited to 2 hours/day, 8 hours/week during school year, 15 hours/week during summer
- Regular check-ins with a Senior Tutor sponsor

## Level 2: College Tutor

> **Terminology note:** Internally and in this pipeline document, these people are called **College Tutors**. In all parent-facing marketing and communications, they are called **College Mentors**. Same people, different framing. See `docs/ServiceTiers.md` for the full positioning and pay structure.

**Who:** Undergraduate college students (or recent graduates)  
**Subjects they teach:** Any subject they are qualified in (assessed)  
**Relationship:** Employee / Contractor  
**Compensation:** Standard hourly rate — see `docs/ServiceTiers.md` for current pay bands

### Requirements

1. **Education:** Currently enrolled in college or recently graduated (within 2 years)
2. **Subject mastery:** Pass MetaMinds subject competency assessments for all subjects they wish to teach
3. **Onboarding:** Complete MetaMinds Tutor Certification (online + live assessment, ~8 hours)
4. **Shadow sessions:** Observe 3 sessions with a Graduate Mentor
5. **Trial period:** First 30 days — all sessions reviewed by a Senior Tutor
6. **Background check:** Required. Full employment verification.
7. **Agreement:** Sign MetaMinds Tutor Agreement (IP ownership of curriculum contributions, confidentiality)

### What College Tutors Teach

College Tutors can teach any subject in their qualified subject list, including:
- SAT / ACT Prep
- High school math through Pre-Calculus
- AP subjects (if they passed the AP exam with a 4 or 5, or received equivalent college credit)
- Programming (Python, Java, Scratch, web development)
- Writing and reading

They **cannot** teach AP Calculus (BC level) without Graduate Mentor approval unless they are a math/engineering major.

### Career Development

College Tutors have access to:
- MetaMinds Curriculum Builder (to contribute lessons, homework, assessments)
- Student analytics dashboard for their students
- Tutor community forum (internal)
- Reference letters from MetaMinds (after 6+ months)
- Priority placement in the Graduate Mentor track

### Advancement to Graduate Mentor

College Tutors may apply for Graduate Mentor status when they:
- Graduate from college
- Have taught 100+ sessions at MetaMinds
- Received average student rating of 4.5+ over last 6 months
- Complete Graduate Mentor training
- Have a professional skill set (engineering, research, entrepreneurship, etc.) approved by admin

## Level 3: Graduate Mentor

**Who:** College graduates + working professionals  
**Subjects / Domains:** Career mentorship, advanced technical skills, research, entrepreneurship  
**Compensation:** Premium hourly rate (significantly higher than College Tutor rate)  

### What Graduate Mentors Offer

Graduate Mentors do not simply teach subjects. They transfer lived experience.

| Domain | Examples |
|--------|---------|
| Software Engineering | Architecture, system design, code review, internship prep |
| Research | Academic writing, lab techniques, grad school applications |
| Entrepreneurship | Business models, pitch decks, fundraising, product development |
| Interview Preparation | FAANG technical prep, behavioral interviews, salary negotiation |
| Career Development | Resume, LinkedIn, networking, job search strategy |
| AP / Advanced Math | Calculus BC, Multivariable, Linear Algebra |
| College Advising | Application strategy, essay review, school selection |

### Requirements

1. **Education:** Bachelor's degree minimum; graduate degree preferred for academic mentorship
2. **Experience:** 2+ years professional experience in their domain
3. **Assessment:** Pass a technical or domain review with MetaMinds academic director
4. **Onboarding:** Complete Graduate Mentor certification (4 hours)
5. **Background check:** Required. Full verification.
6. **Interview:** One-on-one interview with MetaMinds leadership

### The Curriculum Contribution Expectation

Graduate Mentors are expected to contribute at minimum:
- 1 lesson plan per quarter to the knowledge base
- 1 set of practice problems (10 questions minimum) per quarter
- Annual review of their subject area content for accuracy

This is not optional. Curriculum contribution is what makes MetaMinds compound.

- At 10: They learn Scratch from a high schooler who built games
- At 14: They write Python with a college student who is passionate about it

## Messaging Guidelines

**Do say:**
- Junior Mentor
- STEM Coach
- Mentor-in-Training
- MetaMinds Leadership Program

**Do not say:**
- Tutor (for Junior Mentors — implies a different relationship)
- Employee (for Junior Mentors — they are in a program)
- Kids teaching kids (undermines parent trust)
- Cheap tutors (never frame Junior Mentors as a cost-saving measure)
