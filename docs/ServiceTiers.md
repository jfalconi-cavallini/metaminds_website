# MetaMinds Service Tiers

**This document is the canonical reference for service tiers, pricing, tutor pay, and positioning.**

---

## Overview

MetaMinds currently offers two one-on-one tutoring tiers. Both tiers use the same MetaMinds curriculum, session notes, homework system, and parent update workflow. The difference is in tutor experience level and price.

| Tier | Tutor Type | Starting Rate |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Premium Mentoring | Experienced graduates, engineers, professionals | $70/hr |
| College Mentor Tutoring | High-achieving college students | $50/hr |

**Payment rule:** Parents always pay MetaMinds directly. Tutors never collect payment from families under any circumstance.

---

## Premium Mentoring

### Who Qualifies as a Premium Tutor

- College graduates with subject expertise
- Engineers and professionals with STEM backgrounds
- Specialized SAT/ACT instructors
- Tutors with significant classroom or private teaching experience

### Pricing

| Package | Total Price | Effective Per Hour |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 hour | $70 | $70.00 |
| 4 hours | $260 | $65.00 |
| 8 hours | $480 | $60.00 |
| 20 hours | $1,000 | $50.00 |

### Positioning

- Expert instruction from tutors with deep subject mastery and professional teaching experience
- For families who want the most experienced educator available
- Especially appropriate for: advanced SAT prep, AP subjects, specialized STEM, college-level math

---

## College Mentor Tutoring

### Who Qualifies as a College Mentor

High-achieving college students who:
- Are carefully selected and vetted by MetaMinds
- Complete MetaMinds tutor onboarding and training
- Teach under MetaMinds curriculum, systems, and quality-control process
- Are supervised and supported throughout their time on the platform

### Pricing

| Package | Total Price | Effective Per Hour |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 hour | $50 | $50.00 |
| 4 hours | $190 | $47.50 |
| 8 hours | $360 | $45.00 |
| 20 hours | $850 | $42.50 |

### Positioning

College Mentor Tutoring is **not** a budget or lesser option. It is a more accessible tier with the same MetaMinds infrastructure and oversight.

**What is the same:**
- MetaMinds structured curriculum
- Session notes after every session
- Homework assignments with due dates and grading
- Parent progress updates
- Hours package tracking
- Admin oversight

**What is different:**
- Tutor is a college student, not a professional — more relatable for many students
- Lower price point — more sustainable for long-term engagement
- May be especially effective for students who benefit from near-peer mentorship

**How to describe it to parents:**

> "College Mentors are high-achieving students from top universities who tutor under MetaMinds systems, curriculum, and supervision. They bring the full MetaMinds experience — session notes, homework, parent updates, skills tracking — at a more accessible price."

**Never say:** "our cheaper option," "less experienced," "budget tutoring," or any framing that implies lower quality.

## College Mentor Tutor Pay Structure

Proposed pay bands for college tutors (per completed tutoring hour):

| Experience Level | Pay Rate |
|-----------------|----------|
| Newer college tutor | ~$20–$25/hr |
| Experienced or proven college tutor | ~$27.50/hr |
| Exceptional or specialized college tutor | up to $30/hr |

### Founding College Mentor

MetaMinds currently has one active college mentor: a Yale student serving as **Founding College Mentor**.

- Pay rate: **$30/hr** (top of the college mentor pay band)
- Title: Founding College Mentor
- Role includes: tutoring students, SAT/ACT preparation, curriculum feedback, lesson-resource development, future bootcamp involvement

**Title clarification:** "Founding College Mentor" denotes her role as the first college mentor in the program and her early contributions to shaping it. It does not imply company ownership, equity, formal partnership, or co-founder status. MetaMinds is not offering equity or a formal business partnership at this time.

**Enrollment note:** MetaMinds cannot guarantee a fixed number of students or tutoring hours immediately, as enrollment is still building. Hours will be assigned as students enroll and matches are made.

## Premium Tutor Pay

Premium tutor pay rates are not yet formally defined. They will be set before the first premium tutor is matched with a student. Key principle: MetaMinds margin must be sustainable at scale.

## Terminology: "College Mentor" vs. "College Tutor"

These terms refer to the same person but in different contexts:

| Term | Where Used | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **College Mentor** | External — marketing, parent communications, student portal | The customer-facing service tier name |
| **College Tutor** | Internal — `docs/MentorPipeline.md`, `docs/TutorWorkflow.md`, admin portal | The internal pipeline level name |

When writing parent-facing or marketing copy, always use **College Mentor**. When writing internal operations documentation, **College Tutor** is acceptable. Never use "College Tutor" in parent-facing materials — it sounds transactional rather than relational.

## What Is Not Launching Yet

- Stripe Connect or automated tutor payouts
- Recurring group tutoring classes
- Paid bootcamps (first bootcamp is a free community pilot)
- Premium tutor marketplace or matching algorithm
- Automated package deductions (currently manual)

See `docs/Roadmap.md` Phase 0 for the manual workflow that must be proven before automation is built.
